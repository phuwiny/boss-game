# Coin Quest v1.1 — release notes draft

Stage 1 now uses the approved Grassland concept as its playable backdrop, and Memory Match has new character portraits. Gameplay rules and character animation are unchanged.

## Changes

- **Memory Match portraits:** Bobo, Mew, and Aclaire appear on cards as separate transparent pixel-art portraits. Their existing sprites remain visible while a portrait loads or if it fails to load. Bobo has no mouth, Mew has green eyes, and Aclaire has two blue eyes.
- **Grassland background:** Stage 1 draws the mountain, cloud, and meadow portion of `assets/concepts/2026-10-14_scene-concepts.png` as a cached parallax layer. The top edge fades into the sky on tall mobile screens. If the concept image is unavailable, the procedural background appears instead.
- **Preserved gameplay:** The stage layout, collision, coin and enemy placement, physics, and the original four running frames are unchanged. Forest and Castle retain their existing backgrounds.

## Verification

- Chrome visual checks at 844×390 and 390×844 covered Memory Match and Stage 1. A hard Memory deck displayed all three portraits; blocked portrait requests fell back to the old sprites and the 10-pair game could be completed and replayed.
- Normal Stage 1 movement and jumping worked with the concept backdrop. Blocking the concept image restored the procedural scene. Forest and Castle launched without uncaught browser errors.
- `node tools/check-memory.js`, `node --check` for the modified JavaScript, and `git diff --check` passed. The extended `check-level.js` simulation was stopped after its first Grassland/Bobo reachability result, so it is not counted as a complete pass.

Evidence: [portrait and initial Stage 1 QA](2026-10-17_portrait-stage1-qa.md), [concept-background QA](2026-10-17_stage1-concept-background.md). Browser-emulated mobile checks do not establish physical-device performance for v1.1.
